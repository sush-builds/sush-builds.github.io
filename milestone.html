Peak Human Existence

A brief history of my questionable life choices and accidental achievements.

June 26, 2008

The Unfortunate Arrival

I was born. Easily the biggest milestone on this list, mostly because society hasn't managed to recover since. Cried immediately—probably because I realized I’d eventually have to pay taxes and take entrance exams.

2010

Sentence to Institutionalization

Enrolled at Gautam Secondary School, Birgunj, Parsa. Sent off to learn the alphabet and trade lunchboxes while blissfully unaware that this was just Step 1 of a 16+ year academic sentence.

2024

Escaped School, Escaped Birgunj

Conquered the infamous SEE with a 3.40 GPA from Gautam Secondary School. Packed my bags, escaped to Kathmandu, and secured a seat at Trinity International SS, Dillibazar—mostly for the fresh pollution and elevated stress levels.

2026

Peak Academic Flex

Smashed NEB +2 boards with a 3.81 GPA. Proof that late-night panic scrolling and caffeine overload actually work. Peak intellectual performance reached... or so I thought.

Present Day

The CEE Gulag

Currently grinding away, preparing for the CEE. Staring at biology diagrams, questioning every life choice that brought me here, and surviving entirely on caffeine and pure delusion.
